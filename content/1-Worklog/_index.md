## Main Project Direction

The weekly work focuses on an **End-to-End Risk Scoring and Quality Evaluation System for AI Coding Agents on AWS SageMaker**. The completed evidence has two separate tracks: managed Training, evaluation, HPO, Pipeline, Registry, and monitoring acceptance in `us-east-1`; and historical short-lived serving through SageMaker Endpoint, Lambda, API Gateway, Data Capture, and CloudWatch in `ap-southeast-1`. The managed Registry packages remain `PendingManualApproval` and were not deployed.

## Worklog Structure

Each weekly page includes:

- **Objective**: what the week aimed to achieve.
- **Context**: why the work mattered in the project timeline.
- **Technical activities**: concrete implementation or research work.
- **Deliverables**: artifacts or outcomes produced that week.
- **Challenge and solution**: a realistic issue encountered and how it was handled.
- **Evidence and references**: AWS documentation, tutorials, or technical references studied.
